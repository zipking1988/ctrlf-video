## Participant and team eligibility

Every member must be 21+, a working professional, entrepreneur or startup participant, and physically based in and legally resident in JAPAC. Full-time and part-time students are excluded; one ineligible student can disqualify the whole team. Credentials may be checked. [Formal eligibility terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)

Teams require 2–4 members. Solo registration is allowed, solo participation is not. Cross-country JAPAC teams are allowed. Each person joins one team; each team submits one solution under one theme. Participation is free. [Official FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html)

## Technical and submission requirements

The solution must use Google AI models such as Gemini/Gemma or the listed agentic platforms, and deploy through Google Cloud using Cloud Run or Firebase. Submission materials, code and documentation must be English. [Official requirements](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html)

Mandatory submission package:

| Deliverable | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Prototype | Working end-to-end deployed URL |
| Source | Public GitHub repository |
| Demo | Video strictly under three minutes |
| Deck | Architecture and business case; prepare a PDF export |

Sources: [FAQ](https://aibuildercup.com/Faqs.html) and [PDF/public-video instructions](https://aibuildercup.com/themes.html). The requirements page accepts public YouTube, Vimeo or Google Drive video links.

The terms require a primarily Google Cloud implementation. Fresh code/original assets must come from the official event period; pre-existing products, ongoing projects and pre-launch code are excluded. [Formal technical and freshness terms](https://docs.google.com/document/d/e/2PACX-1vRm7ChZ6Ij9fG7uFDkxzUMpwgVeBmnQ6cMDnAIEEX84AiLBOOQ9cYbl3S5OzFBbcVb8TF55s-eVpiXb/pub)

## Awards and finale

The [overview](https://aibuildercup.com/) advertises USD 30,000 total. The [rewards page](https://aibuildercup.com/rewards.html) lists USD 10,000 / 7,000 / 5,000 for the top three, and USD 2,000 each for Best Use of Google Cloud AI Tools, Most Impactful Solution, Social Choice and Best UI/UX.
